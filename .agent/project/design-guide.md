# TheeOrb design guide

## Brand feel

Mysterious, capable, and encouraging: a well-kept pixel arcade workstation for focused career work. The career Orb gives the system a distinct identity, but documents, facts, and explicit human approval remain visually primary.

## Screen hierarchy

| Destination | Primary job |
| --- | --- |
| Job Desk | Paste a listing, review extraction, and create a draft. |
| Applications | Track draft, ready, sent, and failed applications with notes. |
| Career Orb | Configure writing voice, factual boundaries, and approved career evidence. |
| Settings | Career profile, CV document, Gmail connection, account, and privacy controls. |

## Interaction principles

1. **Review before consequence** — extraction precedes drafting; drafting precedes sending; sending has its own final confirmation.
2. **Facts are distinct from suggestions** — show AI-extracted information and user-confirmed profile data with visibly different labels.
3. **Private documents feel protected** — surface owner-only access and attachment state plainly.
4. **Arcade with clarity** — use pixel panels for Job Desk stages, draft status, and career-Orb moments; keep CV forms and email review calm and legible.
5. **One primary action** — each stage has one obvious next action and a visible save-draft or back path.

## Visual rules

- Retain deep black surfaces, electric violet primary actions, and the existing pixel-arcade panel construction.
- Use amber for job extraction, cyan for application history, green for a confirmed send, and pink only for warnings or failures.
- Use the existing rounded dimensional button treatment for every important action and clear disabled/loading states for long-running AI or upload work.
- Pixel typography may lead labels and headings. It must remain legible in forms, email content, and long professional text.
- No emoji structural icons, fake employer branding, or decorative status indicators without real data.

## Accessibility

- Maintain clear labels, field-level errors, keyboard-appropriate inputs, accessible contrast, and 44px touch targets.
- Preserve safe areas and visible back/cancel paths in multi-step workflows.
- Make error recovery explicit for failed uploads, extraction, Gmail connection, and sends.
