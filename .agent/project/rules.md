# TheeOrb product rules

## Product vocabulary

- **Orb:** the member's private career copilot.
- **Nursery:** the private setup space for professional voice, evidence, skills, and application boundaries.
- **Job Desk:** the place to paste and review a job listing.
- **Application:** a saved draft, ready-to-review email, sent application, or delivery failure.
- **Career profile:** the member-approved CV facts, skills, work history, education, links, and contact details.

## Application safety

- The Orb drafts and explains; the member reviews and sends. Never submit or schedule an application automatically.
- Extracted job details are proposals until reviewed. Display missing or uncertain company, pay, requirements, or recipient email clearly.
- Cover letters must only use approved career-profile facts. Do not inflate experience, fabricate achievements, or claim qualifications absent from the profile.
- Keep CVs, profile contact details, Gmail tokens, and application notes private to the owning member.
- Gmail uses only the minimum send scope and must be explicitly connected and disconnectable.
- Use generic public responses for protected resources and log detailed provider failures only on the server.

## UI and copy

- Use direct actions: “Paste listing”, “Review extraction”, “Draft cover letter”, “Connect Gmail”, “Review and send”, and “Save draft”.
- Themed arcade labels may frame a screen but must not obscure CV, job, email, or privacy controls.
- Every setup, upload, extraction, draft, send, and tracker view needs loading, empty, error, draft, and success states where relevant.
- Do not use fake application counts, salary, employer logos, metrics, or success stories.
